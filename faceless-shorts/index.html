Deep Dive 3D

deep-dive

The flagship. Sculpted stylised 3D people in warm sunlit sets, glowing x-ray bodies and camera journeys through blood vessels, with shallow focus and a camera that never stops. Curious, shocking, satisfying.

Best for
Body, health and science “what happens if…?”
Length
30–45 s
Captions
1–3 words, white, one yellow word

The look

  • Three worlds in every video: the real world, the x-ray body, then inside the body, and back out for the ending.
  • Warm low sun outside, cyan inside. X-ray cyan is only for x-ray glass; deep red is for blood.
  • Real three.js 3D: bloom, depth of field, a moving camera on every shot.

Key colours

x-raybloodcaption wordwarm sun

Bundled example: What happens if you hold your breath? · 37 s, 11 shots

Demo: the Alaska treaty signed at 4 a.m. (28–40 s). One lamp, a red-lit subject, typewriter date stamp.

Night File 3D

night-file

Quiet, uneasy, investigative. Dark 3D places lit by one light source, faceless people, typewriter dates and evidence inserts: case files, tickets, polaroids, red circles, film grain, a slow camera.

Best for
Unsolved mysteries, true crime, “this happened in 1971”
Length
30–45 s
Captions
Typewriter, 2–5 words, one red word

The look

  • One light per scene; everything else falls into darkness. Lamps have a soft visible cone.
  • Red means the subject. Witnesses are near-black with a thin red rim; nothing else is red except evidence marks.
  • No faces, no gore, no weapons. Facts checked against FBI, court records or major outlets.

Key colours

darknesssubject redcream typelamp

Bundled example: The man who jumped out of a plane (D.B. Cooper, 1971) · 36 s, 11 shots

Demo: the hook (0–11 s). Flat US map with a 3¢ price tag, the law book, the bird-poop island.

Inside Out

inside-out

Flat 2D “what happens if…?” shorts. A simple flat hero, x-ray cutaways into the body, organs and props as clean vector shapes, bold captions and a cut every 1–3 seconds with the camera always moving.

Best for
A lighter, faster take on body and science topics
Length
25–40 s
Captions
Bold 1–3 words, one yellow word

The look

  • Flat shapes only: no gradients, textures or realistic light. Depth comes from darker and lighter tones of one colour.
  • X-ray cyan only for inside-the-body views. Red marks danger or the problem.
  • Yellow is the one keyword per caption, plus impact bursts.

Palette

skinorgandangerx-rayhighlightbackgroundink

Bundled example: What if you swallow gum? · 30 s, 11 shots

Demo: $7.2 million, Seward's Folly, gold and oil (40–51 s). Stick figure in a snowy Alaska set, gold amount card.

Stick Story

stick-story

Tense, clever, a little funny. Black stick people whose big white eyes do the acting, flat illustrated rooms in day and night versions, gold numbered step cards, one picture per clause.

Best for
Survival, life hacks, “what would you do if…?”
Length
30–45 s
Captions
Dark bar, 2–4 words, one gold word

The look

  • People are always ink black; colour lives in the room. At night they get a soft warm glow.
  • Gold means steps: every step gets a numbered card. Red is danger, green is safe.
  • A cut every 1.5–3 s. The eyes react first, then the body.

Key colours

peoplestep golddangersafeday sky

Bundled example: What if the power went out for 3 days? · 34 s, 12 shots

Demo: $15 million, 3¢ an acre, the map doubles (20–28 s). Cut-paper coins, torn caption strip.

Paper Atlas

paper-atlas

Storybook, curious, grand. Every shape is cut paper on aged parchment with a light paper edge and a soft shadow: maps with crimson routes, gold date ribbons, wax seals, simple period figures.

Best for
Historical events, “how X happened”
Length
30–45 s
Captions
Serif on torn paper, 2–4 words, one crimson word

The look

  • Maps first: where, then who, then what. A gold ribbon opens every new era.
  • Crimson only for routes, targets, fire and the key word; gold only for dates, crowns and seals.
  • No battles shown: tell it with objects, places and consequences.

Key colours

parchmentcrimsongoldnight wash

Bundled example: How one bakery burned down London (1666) · 41 s, 10 shots

Demo: the diplomats ask Napoleon for New Orleans (11–20 s). 2D cartoon people inside a 3D palace with a real French flag.

Tall Tale 3D

tall-tale

Playful, clever, a little cheeky. Flat 2D cartoon people with thick ink outlines, big round white heads and stick limbs, acting inside a sunny, saturated 3D world: small-town streets, wallpapered shops, meadows, trains.

Best for
Funny or clever true stories, cons, loopholes
Length
40–60 s
Captions
One word at a time, white with a black edge

The look

  • People are 2D, the world is 3D. Every person is a cut-out that turns to the camera and casts a real shadow.
  • Faces do the acting: smug, shock, laugh, worried with sweat, changing on the beat.
  • The camera moves in every shot.

Key colours

headsink linemeadowwallpapersky

Bundled example: The girl who was mailed to Grandma (1914) · 52 s, 21 shots

Commands

Make one

Easiest: ask Claude, for example “Make a Night File 3D short about the Dyatlov Pass”. To run it by hand, open Git Bash:

Set the paths once per terminal, so everything stays on G: (C: is nearly full).

export FACELESS_HOME=G:/TechCC/.tools/faceless-home HF_HOME=G:/TechCC/.tools/c-moves/.cache_huggingface TEMP=G:/TechCC/.tools/tmp TMP=G:/TechCC/.tools/tmp FPS=30 PYTHONUTF8=1
SKILL=~/.claude/skills/faceless-shorts; PY=G:/TechCC/.tools/faceless-home/venv/Scripts/python.exe

Start a project from a style's example. Use any slug from above.

bash $SKILL/scripts/new_video.sh deep-dive my-short --dir G:/USA/shorts
cd G:/USA/shorts/my-short

Write script.json (one line per cut, with caps), then make the voice and word timings. List voices with voice.py --list.

$PY $SKILL/scripts/voice.py . && $PY $SKILL/scripts/words.py .

Edit src/shots.js, then check it, look at a contact sheet, and build the sound.

node tools/capture.mjs check
node tools/capture.mjs sheet review/all.jpg 0.5 10
node tools/capture.mjs cues && $PY tools/mix.py .

Render the MP4.

node tools/capture.mjs video renders/my-short.mp4
